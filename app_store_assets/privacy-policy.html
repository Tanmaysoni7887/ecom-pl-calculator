Privacy Policy for Ecom P&L & ROAS Calculator

Effective Date: October 2026

Thank you for using Ecom P&L & ROAS Calculator ("we", "our", or "the App"). We are committed to respecting and protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data collection, usage, and security when you use our mobile application.

Summary: Ecom P&L & ROAS Calculator is an offline-first financial utility. All your calculation inputs (product costs, ad spend, revenue, stores) remain 100% on your device. We do NOT collect, store, or sell your personal or financial data on external servers.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

To ensure full confidentiality for eCommerce sellers and media buyers:

  • We do not collect personally identifiable information (PII) such as your real name, phone number, or home address.
  • We do not access your banking details, UPI IDs, credit/debit card credentials, or payment gateway login secrets.
  • We do not link or read live data from your Meta, Google, or Shopify ad accounts without your explicit action.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The app stores data locally on your smartphone via secure local device storage (AsyncStorage) solely to provide app functionality:

  • Calculated numbers (Selling Price, COGS, Ad Spend, ROAS, Margin history).
  • Custom store profiles, product names, and SKU identifiers.
  • User interface preferences (currency selection, preferred language, theme).

This data never leaves your device and is not synchronized to any third-party cloud server without your initiation.

3. Data Deletion and Control

You have complete control over your data at all times:

  • You can reset or wipe all stored calculations and store data instantly within the app by going to Settings > Clear All Data.
  • Uninstalling the application will permanently remove all locally cached calculations from your device.

4. Third-Party Services and Libraries

The application is built using standard open-source mobile frameworks (Expo / React Native). If basic anonymous crash analytics or store delivery services (e.g. PhonePe Indus Appstore / Google Play Services) are active, they may collect standard device telemetry (device model, OS version, crash logs) under their respective privacy policies to ensure app stability.

5. Children's Privacy

Our app is intended for business, e-commerce entrepreneurs, and adult commercial use (18+). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected with an updated "Effective Date" on this page.
